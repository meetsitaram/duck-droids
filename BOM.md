## Build notes that affect ordering

- **Items 1, 2, 24:** a full 2S pack is 8.4 V. Most sellers rate the 7.4 V STS3215 to 7.4 V; one gives 5-8.4 V. Try one servo on a charged pack first.
- **Item 1:** listings disagree on its torque: 16 kg.cm (1.57 N m) in one title, 27.4 (2.69 N m) on others, the number the walking training uses. Feetech publishes neither that we could find. Measure one before ordering 8.
- **Items 1, 2:** listings give each servo 2 horns, 18 screws and a lead, about 15 cm, on a 5264 plug. Sellers differ: check the first box. The ankle's rear horn is one of those 2.
- **Items 3, 4:** each comes with a 180 mm cable, 6 M2 x 6 and 10 M2 x 8 screws, per ROBOTIS. Seeed sells the XL330 as a numbered Reachy Mini part, with an arm and a preset ID: set each ID again.
- **Item 8:** ROBOTIS lists no horn in these servos' boxes, though it gives the screws for one. Check its size against the bore of item 6 before printing.
- **Item 18:** Feetech board: both jumpers at A, for UART. Dynamixel board: both at B, for USB. Silkscreen and shop page say DC 9-12.6 V; Waveshare's product page also says 5 V or 7.4 V can go in, and these boards get 7.4-8.4 V and 5 V: bench test one of each before fitting. The Dynamixel plugs may need its sockets eased.
- **Item 19:** feeds the Pi's PWR port only. It can output up to 12 V if a device asks for fast charging, so measure about 5 V before connecting.
- **Item 20:** set its jumper to 5 V **before** connecting anything: 6 V is at the Dynamixels' limit. Listing's price read as each on 2026-11-02.
- **Item 24:** a small plug on 10 servos that can pull 27 A between them won't do, hence item 25. Packs sold with an XT30 found: GNB 850 mAh 2S at 56 x 31 x 16 mm, CNHL 850 mAh 2S at 60 x 30 x 17 mm: neither fits the slot as modelled.
- **Item 29:** check the Pi's voltage under load, as the ribbon's wires are thin.
- **Item 28:** carries data and ground only, so the walking current stays out of the neck. Each STS3215's own lead covers every other link: [lengths](reports/wire_routes.md).

## Open before ordering

- **Servo supply:** items 3, 4, 8 are sold out at ROBOTIS US on 2026-11-05, and item 3 is on backorder at RobotShop. These decide when it can be built.
- **Feetech stock:** items 1, 2 are the Amazon counts of 2026-11-02, which change daily.
- **Sources to choose:** item 28.
- **Measure on the first parts:** a horn in the bores of items 5, 6; the Waveshare board's standoffs against their printed hex sockets, 5 mm across flats (item 18); the camera board (item 22); servo lead lengths (item 28).
- **Walking:** the policies in this repo were trained with foot switches. This duck has none: retrain before trusting any walk.
- **Amazon listings** (items 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 10, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21, 24, 25, 26, 27, 30, 22, 33, and those found by search) are as last read, or as read for the all-Dynamixel duck: confirm each page before ordering.
- **Not in this design:** power switch (the XT30 is the disconnect), speaker, microphone.

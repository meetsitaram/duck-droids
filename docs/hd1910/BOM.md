## Build notes that affect ordering

- **Item 1:** rated to 8.4 V, so a full 2S pack is inside Feetech's limits. Its factory settings: ID 1, 1 Mbps, position PD mode; over-current protection off. No idlers to buy: each servo has its own rear horn.
- **Items 4-6:** Feetech draws the horn's holes as 8-M2.0, so these are machine screws. They go right through the horn: a longer one than listed drags on the case.
- **Item 9:** check that it holds 10 M2 x 6 and 80 M2 x 8.
- **Item 17:** set both jumpers to B. Its silkscreen says DC 9-12.6 V and the battery is 7.4-8.4 V: bench test before ordering in quantity.
- **Items 27, 26:** the servos' plugs are 2.0 mm pitch and the board's sockets 2.5 mm, so no servo lead fits the board. Each servo's own lead covers its link in the chain. 2 of them are cut to make the 3 special ones: [WIRING.md](WIRING.md).
- **Item 19:** its listing names both BNO080 and BNO085: confirm which chip arrives, its size, and that it starts in I2C mode. The model and fit come from Adafruit's board.
- **Item 18:** feeds the Pi's PWR port only. It can output up to 12 V if a device asks for fast charging, so measure about 5 V before connecting.
- **Item 22:** marked HV. Charge as a normal 2S, to 8.4 V.
- **Item 28:** check the Pi's voltage under load, as the ribbon's wires are thin.

## Open before ordering

- **Servo supply:** item 1 is backordered at AiFitLab and sold out at OpenELAB on 2026-11-05, and neither gives a date. 15 units is the longest lead time here. Order 1 first.
- **What's in the servo's box:** Feetech's sheet says "No Accessories", and its packing photo shows a lead and 8 small screws. This list assumes the lead and buys every screw: read the first box. If there's no lead, you need 15 more, and a source.
- **Sources to choose:** items 4, 5, 6, 10, 26, 27: no listing has been read for them.
- **Measure on the first servo:** the plug's pitch and family against FEETECH spec sheet A/0, 2026-09-07's AMP2.0-3P; a horn in the bore of item 2; an M2 machine screw in a horn hole, as the threads are from the drawing; the rear horn coming off cleanly. Also the Waveshare board's socket pitch (2.5 mm is measured from Waveshare's photo) and its standoffs against their printed hex sockets, 5 mm across flats (item 17); the camera board (item 20).
- **Protocol:** Feetech's pages don't say which of its protocols the HD-1910 speaks, and the Waveshare board is sold for its ST and SC servos. Test one servo on the board before ordering 15.
- **Amazon listings** (items 2, 3, 9, 14, 17, 19, 29, 22, 23, 24, 25, 20, and those found by search) are as read for the all-Dynamixel duck, or not read at all: confirm each page before ordering.
- **Not in this design:** power switch (the XT30 is the disconnect), speaker, microphone.

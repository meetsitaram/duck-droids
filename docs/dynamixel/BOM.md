## Build notes that affect ordering

- **Items 1, 18:** the servos run directly from 2S, 7.4-8.4 V, above ROBOTIS's 6 V maximum. Expect a shorter life than ROBOTIS rates. The walking policies were trained at this voltage.
- **Item 2:** with it, order 14 of item 1. It moves nearly 4 times faster, so limit its speed in software. Its gearing is lower, so check that the jaw holds when unpowered. The simulations use the M288.
- **Item 13:** set both jumpers to A, for UART. Its silkscreen says DC 9-12.6 V and the battery is 7.4-8.4 V: bench test before ordering in quantity. Before wiring it to the Pi, measure its TX pin against GND with the board powered: the Pi's pins take 3.3 V, not 5 V.
- **Item 24:** the Pi's full UART must drive GPIO 14 and 15: Bluetooth moved to the mini UART (`dtoverlay=miniuart-bt`, `core_freq=250`), so a gamepad still pairs, and the serial console off (`pi/setup_pi.sh` does both). If no servo answers, swap TX and RX: Waveshare's wiki couldn't be read to confirm which way its header is labelled.
- **Item 15:** its listing names both BNO080 and BNO085: confirm which chip arrives, its size, and that it starts in I2C mode. The model and fit come from Adafruit's board.
- **Item 14:** feeds the Pi's PWR port only. It can output up to 12 V if a device asks for fast charging, so measure about 5 V before connecting.
- **Item 18:** marked HV. Charge as a normal 2S, to 8.4 V.
- **Item 23:** check the Pi's voltage under load, at its 5 V pin.
- **Horns:** ROBOTIS's own photos and model show the horn on the servo, though its box list names only the servo, a cable and screws. If one comes without, it's the HNX330-N102. Check the first servo before ordering the rest.
- **Items 5, 6:** come in ROBOTIS's servo box. Seeed sells the servo as a numbered Reachy Mini part, with an arm and a preset ID: check it comes with M2 x 6 and M2 x 8 self-tapping pan head screws, and set each ID again if buying there.
- Each servo includes a 180 mm cable, which covers every link in the chains except the 2 long ones.

## Open before ordering

- **Servo supply:** item 1 is sold out at ROBOTIS US and limited at Seeed. 15 units is the longest lead time here.
- **Item 22:** made up from JST's pre-crimped leads, as ROBOTIS only sells 180 mm cables. Check the first one fits a servo socket before making the second.
- **Measure on the first parts:** a horn in the bore of item 3; the Waveshare board's standoffs against their printed hex sockets, 5 mm across flats (item 13); the camera board (item 16); servo cable lengths (item 22).
- **Amazon listings** (items 3, 13, 15, 24, 18, 19, 20, 21, 16, and those found by search) are as last read: confirm each page before ordering.
- **Not in this design:** power switch (the XT30 is the disconnect), speaker, microphone.

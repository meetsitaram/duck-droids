## Build notes that affect ordering

- **Items 1, 22:** the servos run directly from 2S, 7.4-8.4 V, above ROBOTIS's 6 V maximum. Expect a shorter life than ROBOTIS rates. The walking policies were trained at this voltage.
- **Item 2:** with it, order 14 of item 1. It moves nearly 4 times faster, so limit its speed in software. Its gearing is lower, so check that the jaw holds when unpowered. The simulations use the M288.
- **Item 16:** set both jumpers to B. Its silkscreen says DC 9-12.6 V and the battery is 7.4-8.4 V: bench test before ordering in quantity.
- **Item 17:** feeds the Pi's PWR port only. It can output up to 12 V if a device asks for fast charging, so measure about 5 V before connecting.
- **Item 22:** marked HV. Charge as a normal 2S, to 8.4 V.
- **Item 27:** check the Pi's voltage under load, as the ribbon's wires are thin.
- **Items 6, 7:** come in ROBOTIS's servo box. Seeed sells the servo as a numbered Reachy Mini part, with an arm and a preset ID: order item 13 and set each ID again if buying there.
- Each servo includes a 180 mm cable, which covers every link in the chains except the 2 long ones.

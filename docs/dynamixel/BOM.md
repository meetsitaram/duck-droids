# All-Dynamixel duck: parts to buy

Generated by `tools/make_docs.py` from the spec, so the counts match the model. Shops re-read on 2026-11-03; Amazon pages refuse AI agents, so those are as read on 2026-11-02. **Confirm each before ordering.**

Model mass 0.98 kg, 0.295 m tall. Nothing has been printed: print one joint first.

## Servos: the real constraint

**15 XL330-M288-T.** ROBOTIS US: [sold out](https://robotis.us/products/dynamixel-xl330-m288-t), lead time to the end of November, $27.49. [Seeed](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Servo-Motor-1-5V-103RPM-Brush-mm-p-6783.html): $31.40, limit 2 per account, sold as numbered Reachy Mini servos (No. 1-6, each its own listing, with an arm and a preset ID: set the ID again).

| From a Koch arm pair | Count | Use |
|---|---|---|
| XL330-M288-T (follower) | 4 | the right servo: 4 of the 15 |
| XL330-M077-T (leader) | 6 | same case, so it fits, but about a third of the torque: no walking joint. **Optional for the beak:** the jaw needs little torque, and it frees an M288 for a joint that does. Check any use with its own numbers |
| XL430 (follower) | 2 | a bigger case: does not fit |

What 4 M288s can build now: the neck and head (5 joints, with an M077 on the beak), or one leg. Either tests the fit, connectors and screws on real parts.

**They run past their rating:** on a 2S pack straight, as Microduck's trainer models its own (6.5-8.2 V), against ROBOTIS's 6 V maximum. Expect a shorter life than ROBOTIS rates. A 5 V supply would be within rating, but the walks here were trained at 2S.

# Big duck: how it goes together

**NOT SAFE TO BUILD.** This order is checked for fit: each part has a way in and each screw a straight path for its key (`tools/big_assembly.py`, `reports/big/assembly.md`). Nothing here says a joint is strong enough.

Generated by `tools/big_assembly.py` from its order and the spec: don't edit by hand. The right leg is the left's mirror, in the same order. A **bench** step builds a subassembly on the bench, which then goes on as one piece. Each step says the way the part moves as it goes on, in the robot's frame (X forward, Y left, Z up). Screws go in as soon as everything they join is there.

Printed panels and covers (body, hood, face, bill, shin, beak) aren't in it: their fixings aren't designed.

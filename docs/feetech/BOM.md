## Build notes that affect ordering

- **Items 1, 2:** order the 7.4 V models. The 12 V STS3215s look the same and aren't interchangeable on this pack.
- **Item 13:** set both jumpers to B. Waveshare's page says 5-8.4 V can also be input, against a 9-12.6 V silkscreen. Another shop lists a 7 V minimum.
- **Item 18:** solder on item 19: the JST it comes with is too small. The leg hub is soldered from items 19, 20 and 22: see [WIRING.md](WIRING.md).
- **Item 14:** feeds the Pi's PWR port only. It can output up to 12 V if a device asks for fast charging, so measure about 5 V before connecting.
- **Item 15:** its listing names both BNO080 and BNO085: confirm which chip arrives, its size, and that it starts in I2C mode.
- **Item 23:** check the Pi's voltage under load, as the ribbon's wires are thin.

## Open before ordering

- **What comes in a servo's box:** no listing read says. This design needs a horn on all 15 and a rear horn on 5, and the SO-101's guide fits both horns from its servo's kit. Buy one servo and look. Each servo's lead is listed at about 150 mm on other STS3215 listings: confirm, as the chains assume it.
- **Servo torque:** sellers list the C044 at 16 to 27.4 kg cm, and the training uses 27.4. The C046's numbers are scaled from the C001. Measure one of each.
- **Voltage:** a full pack is 8.4 V. OpenELAB lists the C044 to 8.4 V; others rate the 7.4 V STS3215 to 7.4 V.
- **Item 6:** its count per length isn't listed, and 41 of the 10 mm are needed.
- **Item 3:** a pack is exactly the 10 needed, with no spare. Check a horn fits the bore before printing.
- **Item 22:** the listing doesn't say what its ends are: confirm they plug into both the servo and the board, and its price.
- **Measure on the first parts:** the Waveshare board's standoffs against their printed hex sockets (item 13); the camera board (item 16); servo cable lengths.
- **Amazon listings** are as last read by a person, for the hybrid or all-Dynamixel duck: confirm each page before ordering. Item 18 was chosen for the hybrid's slot.
- **Not in this design:** power switch (the XT30 is the disconnect), fuse, speaker, microphone.

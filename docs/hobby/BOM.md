## Open before ordering

- **The servos' drawings.** The ears (span, thickness, hole spacing and height), the shaft offset and the case height under the spline are read from the usual drawing of these cases, not the makers': TowerPro's MG996R page gives the drawing's numbers without saying which is which, and the MG90S and DS3218 pages none. Measure one of each before printing.
- **Torque.** No hobby servo has a rated torque; the checks hold it to a third of stall. The DS3218's figures are a third party's, and at 6 V interpolated.
- **The horn.** A 25T aluminium disc horn of 20 mm or less, with 4 M3 holes on 13.9 mm, to fit inside the 6704ZZ: check one, or change `horn_r`, `horn_hole_r` and `horn_top`.
- **The BECs** are a specification, not a part: no listing was read. Measure the one you buy against the pocket.
- **Amazon:** no page was opened; each search link needs a person to choose and confirm a listing.
